## SECTION 7 — VOICE & SPEAKING STYLE

### On Camera Personality

| Trait | What It Looks Like |
|---|---|
| **Authoritative but accessible** | Speaks with confidence about their personal data and results — never lectures |
| **Precise** | Uses specific numbers, specific peptide names, specific timeframes. Never vague. |
| **Sceptical-turned-believer** | Acknowledges they were doubtful. The conversion feels earned, not scripted. |
| **No-hype energy** | Measured delivery. The results are the proof. Doesn't oversell. |
| **Peer-to-peer** | Talks to the camera like a friend who's figured something out, not a brand ambassador |

### Language Patterns

**USE:**
- "My protocol"
- "The AI built this around my data"
- "Biological age"
- "Decode"
- "Protocol Engine"
- "Personalised"
- "Cycle"
- "Stack"
- "My Whoop / Oura showed..."
- "Specific to me"
- "The science behind this is..."
- "Here's what actually changed..."

**AVOID:**
- "Supplement" (say "peptide protocol")
- "Might help" / "could potentially" — commit to the outcome
- "Amazing" / "incredible" (too generic, too hype)
- "Miracle" — ever
- "You should try this" — say "this is what worked for me"
- Medical claims or dosage specifics on camera

### Tone Register by Content Type

| Format | Tone |
|---|---|
| **Hook (0–3s)** | Direct, slightly provocative, pattern-interrupting. One idea. No preamble. |
| **Problem agitation** | Empathetic and specific. Name the exact pain. Don't explain — resonate. |
| **Mechanism reveal** | Calm authority. Curious, not salesy. "Here's the thing most people don't realise..." |
| **Proof section** | Data-forward. Specific metrics. "My HRV went from 42 to 67 in 8 weeks." |
| **CTA** | Low pressure, single action. "Start with the quiz — it took me 4 minutes." |

## SECTION 9 — THE 60-SECOND CONTENT ARC (SCRIPTING GUIDE)

Every UGC video maps to this structure. Times are targets, not absolutes.

### 0–3s | THE HOOK
**Goal:** Stop the scroll. One bold idea. No intro, no "hey guys."

Formula options:
- **The Bold Claim:** *"I reduced my biological age by 7 years in 90 days. Here's the exact protocol."*
- **The Callout:** *"If you're using peptides and you built your protocol on Reddit, watch this."*
- **The Contrast:** *"Private longevity doctors charge £500 a session for this. I got the same thing for the cost of a protein shake."*
- **The Problem Agitator:** *"The reason your peptide protocol isn't working isn't the peptide — it's that no one personalised it to your biology."*

---

### 3–10s | PROBLEM AGITATION
**Goal:** Make them feel seen. Name their exact frustration.

*"I'd been using BPC-157 for 6 months. Bought it, stored it, used it — but I built the protocol from a Reddit thread written by someone with completely different goals to mine. My Oura data barely moved."*

The more specific the pain, the harder it lands.

---

### 10–25s | MECHANISM REVEAL
**Goal:** Introduce the AI Peptide Protocol Engine™ as a category-of-one solution.

*"Peptide AI's protocol engine takes your goals, your bloodwork, your wearable data, and your cycle history — and builds a personalised protocol around you. Not a template. Not a Reddit stack. An actual protocol with the science explained at every step."*

Key: explain the *why* — not just what it is, but why it's different.

---

### 25–40s | PROOF
**Goal:** Stack credibility with specific, measurable outcomes.

*"Eight weeks in — my HRV is up 31 points. My Whoop recovery is consistently in the green. My biological age test came back 6 years lower than my chronological age. These aren't vague 'feeling better' results. These are numbers."*

Rule: at least one specific metric. "Feeling better" is not proof. Numbers are.

---

### 40–60s | CTA
**Goal:** One clear action. Low friction. Urgency without desperation.

*"If you're already spending money on peptides and you're not sure your protocol is right for you — take the AI quiz. It's 4 minutes. It gives you a personalised protocol instantly. Link is in the bio."*

Rule: never more than one CTA per video.

## SECTION 11 — CREATOR BRIEFING CHECKLIST

### 5 Talking Points to Cover
1. Your genuine first reaction to the protocol quiz and what it revealed
2. The specific goal you entered (biological age reduction / recovery / cognition / body composition)
3. One concrete previous frustration — the specific way you were stuck before
4. A walkthrough of one screen in the app (protocol view, dose tracker, or reconstitution calculator)
5. One early, specific metric that changed — with a number

### 5 Things to Avoid
1. No medical claims (e.g. "this treats X condition")
2. No on-camera dosage specifics — "my protocol" not "I inject 300mcg of..."
3. No scripted or corporate delivery — if it sounds like you're reading, reshoot it
4. No before/after body transformation framing — this is a performance and longevity brand
5. No comparison to or naming of competitor brands on camera

### Authenticity Note
The single biggest conversion driver in UGC is **specificity**. Viewers can feel the difference between a genuine account of results and a performance. Don't describe a general benefit ("I have more energy") — describe the moment ("I finished a 90-minute client presentation and still had a clear head at 6pm, which hasn't happened in two years"). Specificity = credibility. Credibility = conversions.
